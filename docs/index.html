Daily Brilliancy Uploader

Daily Brilliancy Uploader is a private tool used by the owner of the Daily_Brilliancy chess Shorts channel to publish their own short chess videos to their own YouTube channel and TikTok account, and to add a single comment ("Why is this move brilliant?") under each of their own videos.

The tool is not offered to the public and is only used with the owner's own accounts.
